In essence, Latent Semantic Indexing/Analysis is just the application of Singular Value Decomposition on the document-term matrix, thus obtaining a lower dimension representation of each document. Similar to the recommender systems section, SVD is used to give us 3 matrices. The first two can be combined into the resulting embedding of the document-term matrix, which is sort of the mixture of topics, and the other matrix being the probability distribution of words for each topic. Note the similarity with MF and other similar methods used in NLP.
