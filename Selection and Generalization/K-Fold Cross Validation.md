We split the dataset into K parts. Then we omit one of the parts and train on the rest of the data. The omitted part is then used to test the model. Finally this procedure is repeated for every one of the parts. Then we can obtain a better estimate of the model's classification rate by taking the mean and variance of the rates obtained at each iteration. This methodology does increase training time by a lot since the models have to be trained K times, but it allows us to better understand the model's performance and allows us to analyse it using statistical techniques and tests such as a T-test.
