Here we discuss some basic definitions of commonly used words in NLP nomenclature.
- <span style="color:rgb(133, 101, 230)">Token</span>: Tokens can be words, punctuation or even sub-units of words. Thus a sentence may be considered a sequence of tokens.
- <span style="color:rgb(133, 101, 230)">Vocabulary</span>: The set of 'all' words, in practice just a reasonable subset of words.
- <span style="color:rgb(133, 101, 230)">Corpus</span>: Corpus refers to a collection of writings of a specific kind or on a specific subject. In NLP this tends to refer to the dataset we are using.
- <span style="color:rgb(133, 101, 230)">N-Gram</span>: Refers to a list of N consecutive items. So 3-gram might be a 3 word expression, or perhaps 3 characters. There are also special names for some N-grams, unigram, bigram, trigram.
- <span style="color:rgb(133, 101, 230)">Bag of Words</span>: Refers to representations where the order of the words does not matter, i.e. results in the same vector regardless of order. Clearly these representations lose information.
